@ goldsmith | The Goldsmith Who Made Tools
golden_pickaxe@ruined_portal | The Pick He Was Laughed At For | Ferro the goldsmith made tools of gold, which everybody knows is foolish. "They break," said the miners. "Yes," said Ferro, "but have you ever seen anything so pretty break?"
golden_boots@ruined_portal | The Slippers for the Princess | The princess ordered golden slippers to dance once at her coronation. She danced once. Ferro had made them to last exactly one dance. They lasted exactly one dance.
golden_chestplate@ruined_portal | The Plate That Fed No One | Ferro's apprentice asked him why. Ferro said a farmer worked his whole life in ugly clothes, and once, just once, he should wear something beautiful while he did it. He gave this to a farmer.
golden_axe@ruined_portal | The Last Commission | His last piece: a golden axe for his own coffin, "so I can chop my way out if I change my mind". They buried it with him. Then a portal opened under the graveyard, and here it is.

@ deepspear | The Spear of the Drowned Princess
diamond_spear@end_city | The Spear That Went Up | A princess of the sea once threw a diamond spear so high it never came down. Sailors say it reached the islands in the sky. Sailors say a lot. This time, apparently, sailors were right.
leather_chestplate@buried | Her Old Coat | Before the diamond spear, she wore this to swim out to the reef. She buried it on an island with a map, so that one day a child would find it and want to be a princess of the sea too.
stone_spear@shipwreck | Her First Spear | Before all that, she had this: a stone point bound to a stick with seaweed, made when she was six. She speared her first fish with it. The fish, to be fair, had been dead a while.

@ chaindad | My Father's Chainmail
chainmail_helmet | His Helmet | My father wore chainmail because he couldn't afford iron, and told everyone he preferred it "for the breeze". He said it so often that by the end he believed it. So did I.
chainmail_chestplate | His Coat of Rings | He mended it himself, ring by ring, at the kitchen table at night. I'd fall asleep to the little clinks. I still can't sleep in a quiet house.
chainmail_leggings | His Leggings | He took me fishing in these, which is a terrible thing to wear fishing. He fell in. He sank like an anchor. He came up laughing, with a fish in his hands and weed on his head.
chainmail_boots | His Boots, My Boots | They fit me now. I wear them on the anniversary and walk his old route along the wall. They clink at every step. People in the village say "morning" without looking up, like they used to.

@ woodcarver | The Old Woodcarver of Hollin
wooden_axe@village | The Carving Axe | Old Hollin carved every toy for every child in the village, for fifty years, with this tiny axe. Wooden knights, wooden dragons, wooden cows with too many legs because he was tired.
leather_chestplate | The Coat She Made Him | His wife made him a proper coat for their thirtieth anniversary. He never wore it. He said it was too nice. It hung by the fireplace next to her picture, and he carved beside it every night.
iron_axe | The Axe of the Last Toy | His last carving: a little wooden figure of a man with a pipe, sitting on a bench, watching children play. Everyone knew who it was. It sits on the bench in the square now, in the rain.

@ spearfisher | The Spear-Fishers of Kelp Point
copper_spear | Old Brin's Copper | At Kelp Point they still fish with spears, standing in the shallows at dawn, the way their grandmothers did. Old Brin's spear is green as the water and twice as old as she is.
iron_spear | Young Tam's Iron | Tam is twelve and has never caught a fish. He stands in the water every morning anyway, very still, very serious. The fish are very fond of Tam. They swim around his ankles.
leather_boots | The Teaching Boots | Every child at Kelp Point gets a stone spear first, so it doesn't matter if they throw it into the deep, and old boots, so it doesn't matter if they fall in after it. The fish build houses with the spears.

@ diamondtools | Mira Stoneheart's Shortcut
diamond_pickaxe | Mira's Pick | Mira Stoneheart was the best miner in the mountains and the worst at keeping her diamonds. She gave most of them away. This pick she kept, because it was the first thing she ever made that was perfect.
diamond_boots | Mira's Tunnel Boots | She dug a tunnel through the mountain in these, so the children of the far valley could get to school in winter. It took her nine years. The tunnel is still called Mira's Shortcut.
diamond_helmet | Mira's Helmet | She wore it cutting the beams for the tunnel, one by one, with a child's name carved into each: every child who'd walk through. There are two hundred and twelve names.
diamond_hoe | Mira's Garden Hoe | When she was old she stopped digging and grew strawberries by the tunnel entrance, for the children walking home. She charged one joke per strawberry. The jokes were terrible. The strawberries were excellent.

@ courtfool | The King's Fool
golden_helmet | The Fool's Crown | A jester's cap made of gold, because the king said only the fool was allowed to make fun of the crown, so the fool should have one too. The fool wore it backwards. The king laughed. Nobody else dared.
leather_boots | The Fool's Dancing Shoes | He danced on the banquet table for forty years and never once spilled the soup. The king's guards once tried to dance with him. Three of them are still in the infirmary.
iron_sword | The Fool's Wooden Sword (Iron) | His prop sword, painted to look like wood. Only the fool knew it was real iron. On the night of the assassins he was the only one in the hall who was armed. He didn't tell a single joke that night.
shield | The Fool's Last Joke | When the king died, the fool was the only one who didn't cry at the funeral. He told the king's favourite joke, over the grave, very badly, on purpose. The whole hall laughed until it cried.
