@ galen | Sir Galen's Borrowed Chainmail
chainmail_helmet | The Helm Galen Couldn't Afford | Galen was the poorest knight in the order. His helm was borrowed from a cousin, his oath was borrowed from his father, and his courage, he said, was borrowed from his mother, who had a lot of it.
chainmail_chestplate | The Rattling Coat | The other knights laughed at his chainmail. It rattled. It rusted. It let the rain in. He polished it every night anyway, and he was always first in line.
chainmail_leggings | The Kneeling Leggings | At the siege of Greyford he knelt beside a wounded enemy soldier in the mud and held his hand until it was over. The other knights stopped laughing at Galen after that.
chainmail_boots | The Boots That Stood | When the gate broke, Galen stood in it. The knights in their shining plate stood behind him. Afterwards they said it was the bravest thing they'd seen. Galen said the chainmail did most of it.
iron_sword | Galen's Sword, Returned | The cousin got his helm back with a letter: "He wore it well. He wore everything well. The order is having a new suit made in his name, in chainmail, for the poorest knight who joins."

@ doctor | Doctor Wren of the Lower Valley
leather_helmet | The Doctor's Cap | When the fever came to the lower valley, everyone who could leave left. Doctor Wren put on her cap, rolled up her sleeves and walked the other way, down into it.
leather_chestplate | The Long Coat | Seventy days she wore this coat, from house to house. She slept in it, in chairs, by beds. The pockets are full of little notes: names, and next to each name, a tick, or a small cross.
shears | The Bandage Shears | She cut a thousand bandages with these. The handles are worn smooth where her thumb rested. Near the end, she cut her own.
flint_and_steel | The Fire for the Water | Every water the valley drank, she boiled first, on fires lit with this flint. That was what ended the fever, in the end. Boiled water, patience and one stubborn woman.
iron_shovel | The Garden on the Hill | When it was over, the valley planted a garden on the hill, a flower for every name with a tick. It is a very big garden. The doctor's cap hangs on the gate.

@ bandit | The Bandit Queen of Copper Ridge
copper_sword | Red Mag's Blade | Red Mag robbed only the rich, and only on Tuesdays, and only after a polite letter of warning. The rich were furious about the letters. "Stealing is one thing. But warning us is just rude."
copper_spear | The Bridge-Keeper's Spear | Her right hand, Big Tobble, held the bridge with this. Nobody crossed without paying the toll. The toll was a joke. If it was funny, you crossed for free. Tobble had no sense of humour.
copper_chestplate | The Green Coat | She wore copper because it went green, and green, she said, was the colour of the forest, and the forest was hers. The forest was not hers. The forest put up with her.
copper_boots | The Escape Boots | The king's soldiers chased her to the edge of the ridge. She jumped. They looked down. She was gone. She'd landed in a haystack she'd put there three years before, just in case.
bow | Mag's Last Arrow | In the end she was pardoned, because she had robbed the king's tax collector and given it all back to the villages, and the villages loved her more than they loved the king. The king noticed.

@ golemfriend | Lina and the Iron Giant
iron_helmet | The Helmet Lina Made It | Lina was six when she climbed onto the iron golem's shoulders and put a helmet on its head, to keep the rain off. It let her. It was the only thing in the village it ever let touch it.
iron_chestplate | The Breastplate She Polished | Every Sunday she polished the golem with an old rag and a lot of spit. It stood very still, the way a dog does when you scratch the right spot. It gave her a poppy every time. She has a box full.
iron_leggings | The Night of the Raid | When the raid came, the golem stood in front of Lina's house and didn't move, while the whole village burned around it. Lina's house was the only one still standing in the morning. The golem wasn't.
iron_boots | What Was Left | They found these where it fell, standing upright, as if it was still on guard. Lina was eleven. She sat with the pieces for three days and nobody could make her come in.
iron_boots | The New One | The village built a new golem. Lina, grown, put the old boots on its feet herself. The new golem looked down at her for a long time. Then it handed her a poppy.

@ sixtyyears | Sixty Years at Cobbleford
golden_helmet | His Wedding Hat | He married her in a golden helmet because he was nervous and wanted to look like someone braver. She laughed through the whole ceremony. He said that was the moment he knew.
diamond_boots | Her Walking Boots | She walked everywhere, all her life, fast, with him half a step behind, saying "wait". For sixty years she never once waited. For sixty years he always caught up.
iron_shovel | Their Garden Spade | They argued about the garden for sixty years: her roses, his potatoes. In the end the garden was half and half, and the potatoes grew between the roses, and it was the best garden in Cobbleford.
fishing_rod | His Last Fishing Trip | After she was gone he went fishing at their spot, and walked there fast, the way she did, saying "wait" to nobody. He caught nothing. He said she'd have caught three. She would have.
